A topic is **mastered** when you solve its mastery-check problem with **no hints** and then **explain it in your own
words**. Each topic page ends with a *Your mastery check* card. Tick it there, and everything shows up below.

<Callout type="info" title="No account needed">

Your progress is saved **only in this browser** (nothing is sent to any server, so nobody else can see it). That
means it's per person and per device: use **Export** below to keep a backup or to move to another device.

</Callout>
